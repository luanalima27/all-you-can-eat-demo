    What is drawn. The 455 users in the 140-core of the network of users who both review and publish (section 3 of the analysis), placed by a force layout of all their reviews. Colours are the two robust communities from 20 Leiden runs. Moving lines are the reviews they wrote to each other in the shown month; small dots flying in are reviews they received from users outside the circle (one dot per four reviews). Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to move, and click a member to follow them. Node size grows with the reviews a member received over all years; brightness shows recent activity.

    Data. Food.com Recipes and User Interactions (Majumder et al., 2019): 1,130,473 reviews without self-reviews, January 2002 to October 2018. Forum games are detected from keywords in review text (ZWT, Pick A Chef, Photo Tag…), so their share is a minimum.
